02 A long dream, a shorter wake. read · 3 min

The picture opens with a jitterbug and closes with a whisper. Between those bookends Lynch builds a Los Angeles that behaves the way cities behave inside our dreams — recognizable, imprecise, kinder than it should be and then suddenly not. There is a blue box, a cowboy, an audition scene that rearranges the floor beneath you, and a diner on Sunset whose back alley contains something you absolutely do not want to see.

“It'll be just like in the movies. We'll pretend to be someone else.”

The best way in is to stop trying to solve it. The film rewards attention the way a painting rewards attention: not with an answer, but with a stranger, longer version of the question. Come early. Stay for the second half.
